You've already used basic <Code>WHERE</Code> in the Read/SELECT lesson. This goes deeper — combining multiple conditions correctly, understanding operator precedence, and sorting with more control.

We'll use an expanded <Code>books</Code> table:

```sql title="create-table.sql"
CREATE TABLE books (
    id INT AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
    title VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    author VARCHAR(255),
    genre VARCHAR(100),
    price DECIMAL(6,2),
    published_year INT,
    in_stock BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE
);
```

## 1. Combining conditions with AND / OR

<Code>AND</Code> requires both conditions to be true. <Code>OR</Code> requires at least one.

```sql title="and-example.sql"
-- Both conditions must be true
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE genre = 'Sci-Fi' AND in_stock = TRUE;
```

<Output>
+----+-------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title | author        | genre  | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  1 | Dune  | Frank Herbert | Sci-Fi | 15.50 |           1965 |        1 |
+----+-------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

---

```sql title="or-example.sql"
-- Either condition can be true
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE genre = 'Dystopian' OR genre = 'Satire';
```
<Output>
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title           | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  2 | 1984            | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm     | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
|  4 | Brave New World | Aldous Huxley | Dystopian | 10.25 |           1932 |        1 |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 2. Operator precedence — the mistake almost everyone makes

<Code>AND</Code> is evaluated **before** <Code>OR</Code>, the same way multiplication happens before addition in math. This can produce surprising results if you're not careful.

```sql title="example.sql"
-- Looks like: "Orwell books, OR anything published after 1950 that's Sci-Fi"
-- But this actually runs as: (author = 'George Orwell') OR (genre = 'Sci-Fi' AND published_year > 1950)
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE author = 'George Orwell' OR genre = 'Sci-Fi' AND published_year > 1950;
```

**Fix: use parentheses to make your intent explicit.**

```sql title="example.sql"
-- Now it's unambiguous: Orwell books published after 1950, in Sci-Fi genre
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE (author = 'George Orwell' OR genre = 'Sci-Fi') AND published_year > 1950;
```

**Rule of thumb:** any time you mix <Code>AND</Code> and <Code>OR</Code> in the same <Code>WHERE</Code>, add parentheses — even if you're confident about precedence. It makes the query readable for anyone else (including future you).

## 3. NOT

Inverts a condition:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE NOT genre = 'Sci-Fi';

-- Equivalent, and more common in practice
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE genre != 'Sci-Fi';
```

<Output>
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title           | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  2 | 1984            | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm     | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
|  4 | Brave New World | Aldous Huxley | Dystopian | 10.25 |           1932 |        1 |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

<Code>NOT</Code> is more useful combined with other operators:

##### Books NOT published in the Sci-Fi or Dystopian genres:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE genre NOT IN ('Sci-Fi', 'Dystopian');
```

<Output>
+----+-------------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title       | author        | genre  | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-------------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  3 | Animal Farm | George Orwell | Satire |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
+----+-------------+---------------+--------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 4. Filtering with BETWEEN

Inclusive range check — includes both endpoints:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE published_year BETWEEN 1940 AND 1960;
```

Equivalent to:
```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE published_year >= 1940 AND published_year <= 1960;
```

<Output>
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title       | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  2 | 1984        | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
|  5 | Foundation  | Isaac Asimov  | Sci-Fi    | 12.00 |           1951 |        0 |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 5. Filtering with IN

Matches against a list — much cleaner than chaining several <Code>OR</Code> conditions:

**Instead of this:**
```sql
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE author = 'George Orwell' OR author = 'Isaac Asimov';
```

**Write this:**
```sql
SELECT * FROM books
WHERE author IN ('George Orwell', 'Isaac Asimov');
```

<Output>
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title       | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  2 | 1984        | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
|  5 | Foundation  | Isaac Asimov  | Sci-Fi    | 12.00 |           1951 |        0 |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 6. Handling NULL correctly

<Code>NULL</Code> means "unknown" or "missing" — it can't be compared with <Code>=</Code>. This trips up almost everyone at some point.

**WRONG — this never matches, even if genre is actually NULL:**
```sql
SELECT * FROM books WHERE genre = NULL;
```

**CORRECT:**
```sql
SELECT * FROM books WHERE genre IS NULL;
SELECT * FROM books WHERE genre IS NOT NULL;
```

## 7. Sorting with multiple columns

The second column only matters when the first has ties:

#### Sort by genre alphabetically, then cheapest first within each genre:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT * FROM books
ORDER BY genre ASC, price ASC;
```

<Output>
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title           | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  2 | 1984            | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  4 | Brave New World | Aldous Huxley | Dystopian | 10.25 |           1932 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm     | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
|  5 | Foundation      | Isaac Asimov  | Sci-Fi    | 12.00 |           1951 |        0 |
|  1 | Dune            | Frank Herbert | Sci-Fi    | 15.50 |           1965 |        1 |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 8. Sorting by a calculated expression

You can sort by something that isn't even a plain column:

#### Sort by how many years since publication (oldest first):

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT title, published_year
FROM books
ORDER BY (2026 - published_year) DESC;
```
<Output>
+-----------------+----------------+
| title           | published_year |
+-----------------+----------------+
| Brave New World |           1932 |
| Animal Farm     |           1945 |
| 1984            |           1949 |
| Foundation      |           1951 |
| Dune            |           1965 |
+-----------------+----------------+
</Output>

## 9. LIMIT and OFFSET
Limits the number of rows returned, and can be used with <Code>OFFSET</Code> to paginate results.

#### Return the first 3 rows:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT *
FROM books
LIMIT 3;
```
<Output>
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title       | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  1 | Dune        | Frank Herbert | Sci-Fi    | 15.50 |           1965 |        1 |
|  2 | 1984        | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |           1949 |        1 |
|  3 | Animal Farm | George Orwell | Satire    |  7.50 |           1945 |        0 |
+----+-------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

#### Return the next 2 rows:

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT *
FROM books
LIMIT 2 OFFSET 3;
```
<Output>
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
| id | title           | author        | genre     | price | published_year | in_stock |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
|  4 | Brave New World | Aldous Huxley | Dystopian | 10.25 |           1932 |        1 |
|  5 | Foundation      | Isaac Asimov  | Sci-Fi    | 12.00 |           1951 |        0 |
+----+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+----------------+----------+
</Output>

## 10. Putting it together

```sql title="example.sql"
SELECT title, author, genre, price
FROM books
WHERE (genre = 'Sci-Fi' OR genre = 'Dystopian') AND in_stock = TRUE
ORDER BY price ASC
LIMIT 3;
```

**Read it like a sentence:** "Give me title, author, genre, price from books where the genre is Sci-Fi or Dystopian, and it's in stock, sorted cheapest first, top 3 only."

<Output>
+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+
| title           | author        | genre     | price |
+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+
| 1984            | George Orwell | Dystopian |  9.99 |
| Brave New World | Aldous Huxley | Dystopian | 10.25 |
| Dune            | Frank Herbert | Sci-Fi    | 15.50 |
+-----------------+---------------+-----------+-------+
</Output>
